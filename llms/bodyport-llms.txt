# Bodyport

> Bodyport is a healthcare technology company delivering remote heart-failure monitoring. Its FDA-cleared Cardiac Scale measures hemodynamic biomarkers through a patient's feet in under 30 seconds and transmits the data over cellular to care teams. The Congestion Index algorithm combines those biomarkers to detect worsening heart failure earlier than weight-based rules, surfaced through a connected clinical dashboard and patient portal.

This profile is maintained by API Evangelist (apis.io). Bodyport does not publish a public developer API, OpenAPI specification, SDK, or developer portal; the entries below are the company's public product, portal, and compliance surfaces.

## Products
- [Bodyport Cardiac Scale](https://www.bodyport.com/): FDA-cleared at-home scale that captures hemodynamic biomarkers through the feet in under 30 seconds.
- [Congestion Index](https://www.bodyport.com/): Algorithm combining hemodynamic biomarkers to detect worsening heart failure.
- [Care Platform](https://www.bodyport.com/): Connected clinical dashboard and patient portal for monitoring, alerts, and care coordination.

## Portals
- [Clinical Dashboard](https://clinical.bodyport.com/): Care-team login for monitoring and alerts (authenticated single-page application).
- [Patient Portal](https://care.bodyport.com/): Patient-facing login (authenticated).

## Compliance
- FDA 510(k) cleared (Cardiac Scale)
- HIPAA compliant
- SOC 2 certified
